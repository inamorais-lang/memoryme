3. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): creating pictures, keeping your account, credits and history.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): keeping purchase records.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): security of the service and abuse prevention.

4. Artificial intelligence

Pictures and lines are created by artificial intelligence and carry the mark "Made with AI · PetBubble". The AI checks that the photo shows a cat or a dog, transforms the picture and writes the line. No automated decisions with legal effects are made about you. Please use photos of your pet: if people appear in them, they are also sent to the services below.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and object to its processing, by writing to [email to be set]. We reply within one month.

Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete account. It immediately deletes your account, photos, pictures and credits. Purchase records are kept only for the legal period.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in Portugal the CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).

7. Age

PetBubble is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, you need your parents' permission to make purchases.

8. Security

Data is sent encrypted. Photos are kept in private folders only you can access. The keys of the AI services stay on the server only.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
