3. No biometric data

We do not collect biometric identifiers or biometric information, do not perform facial recognition, and do not extract face geometry from photos. The AI only checks whether a photo shows a cat or a dog and where the animal's head is, so the bubble does not cover it.

4. Legal bases (EU/UK)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): creating pictures, keeping your account, credits and history.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): sending your photo to the AI providers. You give it before your first picture and can withdraw it in Settings; withdrawing does not affect processing already done.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): keeping purchase records.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): security of the service and abuse prevention.

5. Artificial intelligence

Pictures and lines are created by AI and carry the mark "Made with AI · PetBubble". The AI checks that the photo shows a cat or a dog, transforms the picture and writes the line. No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made about you. Please use photos of your pet: if people appear, their image is also sent to the AI providers.

7. Your rights (EU/UK)

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, object to its processing, and withdraw consent, by writing to petbubble.ai@icloud.com. We reply within one month.

  • Delete a picture: on the picture screen, "Delete picture" removes it from your history and our servers.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. It immediately deletes your account, photos, pictures and credits. Purchase records are kept only for the legal period. Copies with AI providers are deleted under their retention terms (section 6).
  • You can complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal the CNPD (www.cnpd.pt), in Spain the AEPD (www.aepd.es), in France the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), in the UK the ICO (ico.org.uk), or the authority where you live.

8. United States residents

In the last 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (Apple account identifier, email if shared, IP address), commercial information (purchases and credits), user content (photos, pictures, text you type) and internet activity (technical logs). We use them only for the purposes in section 2 and disclose them only to the service providers in section 6. We do not sell or share your personal information (as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, CCPA/CPRA), do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and do not process sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to obtain a copy. Email petbubble.ai@icloud.com; we will verify your request by your Apple account and respond within 45 days. You may use an authorised agent. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our answer. The app does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because we do not track you across apps or websites.

9. Children

PetBubble is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). If you believe a child has used the app, write to petbubble.ai@icloud.com and we will delete the data.

10. Security

Data is sent encrypted and stored encrypted. Photos are kept in private folders only you can access. The AI providers' keys stay on the server. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
